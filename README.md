SparkleCore AI Cleaning Operations Assistant
1. Project Overview
SparkleCore Cleaning Services is a cleaning business concept serving Johannesburg, South Africa. The business plans to begin with residential cleaning and grow into office and institutional cleaning as its capacity expands.
This project uses a carefully designed AI prompt to support the day-to-day work of the business. The SparkleCore AI Cleaning Operations Assistant is intended to help organise cleaning jobs, prepare customer communications, summarise meetings, research cleaning-related questions, and provide consistent responses to common workplace enquiries.
The assistant is a support tool for the business owner and team. It does not replace human judgement, on-site assessments, or confirmation of prices and bookings.
2. Features
Smart Email Generator: Drafts professional customer emails, including enquiry replies, booking follow-ups, and service updates.
AI Cleaning Task Planner and Scheduler: Helps organise cleaning tasks, priorities, job requirements, and proposed schedules.
Meeting Notes Summariser: Turns meeting notes into concise summaries, decisions, and action items.
AI Research Assistant: Helps research cleaning methods, equipment, supplies, customer-service practices, and business operations.
AI Workplace Chatbot: Answers common internal questions and helps staff find clear, consistent guidance.
Client and Job Management Support: Helps structure customer details and job information for planning. Sensitive information should only be entered into approved systems.
Responsible AI Safeguards: Encourages the assistant to be transparent about uncertainty, protect confidential information, avoid invented prices or availability, and refer important decisions to a person.
Implementation note: These are the intended capabilities described by the project prompt. A feature should only be described as fully implemented after it has been built and tested in the chosen platform.

6. Responsible Use
Do not invent prices, service availability, quotations, or booking confirmations.
Confirm schedules and important customer commitments with the business before sending them.
Keep customer and employee information confidential, and avoid entering sensitive personal information into unapproved AI tools.
Check AI-generated cleaning, safety, and operational advice before using it in real work.
Treat AI output as a draft or recommendation that a responsible person must review.
